# Ofgem Great British Insulation Scheme (GBIS) Guidance Summary

## [GBIS-ELIG-01] Great British Insulation Scheme (GBIS) General & Low-Income Routes
Ofgem GBIS funds single insulation measures for EPC D-G homes in Council Tax bands A-D (England) or A-E (Scotland and Wales) under the general group, and low-income/means-tested households in EPC D-G regardless of Council Tax band.

## [GBIS-TECH-02] GBIS Loft Top-Up and Cavity Wall Single Measures
Under GBIS, properties with loft insulation below 100mm qualify for loft top-up to 270-300mm, and properties with unfilled cavity walls qualify for cavity wall insulation with low-cost customer contribution.
