# BSI PAS 2035:2023 Retrofitting Dwellings for Improved Energy Efficiency

## [PAS2035-VENT-01] PAS 2035 Ventilation & Moisture Risk Assessment
BSI PAS 2035:2023 mandates checking existing background, purge, and extract ventilation before installing insulation measures so airtightness gains do not trigger condensation, damp, or mould; compliant trickle vents or MEV/dMEV must be specified where inadequate.

## [PAS2035-RISK-02] PAS 2035 Retrofit Coordinator & Risk Path Classification
Every ECO4 and GBIS installation requires a TrustMark-registered Retrofit Coordinator to assign Risk Path A, B, or C based on building age (e.g., pre-1919 solid wall), exposure, and measure interactions, plus post-installation lodgement in TrustMark Data Warehouse.
