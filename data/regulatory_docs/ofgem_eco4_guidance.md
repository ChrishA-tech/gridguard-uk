# Ofgem ECO4 Statutory Guidance Summary (2022–2026)

## [ECO4-ELIG-01] ECO4 Owner-Occupied & Private Rented EPC Band Thresholds
Under Ofgem ECO4 (2022-2026), owner-occupied homes must hold EPC band D, E, F, or G and meet low-income or vulnerable-household criteria (or LA Flex). Private rented sector homes are eligible in bands E, F, or G (or D for specified innovation/flex routes).

## [ECO4-ELIG-02] ECO4 Social Housing & Minimum Band Improvement
Social housing properties in EPC bands E, F, or G qualify under ECO4 for fabric-first insulation and heating upgrades, subject to minimum SAP improvement targets (bands F/G to at least D; bands D/E to at least C) and TrustMark lodgement.

## [ECO4-FLEX-03] ECO4 Local Authority Flexible Eligibility (LA Flex)
Local Authorities may refer households under ECO4 Flex Route 1 (gross annual income under £31,000), Route 2 (IMD decile 1-3 proxy indicators plus health/vulnerability), or Route 3 (NHS health referral) for EPC D-G dwellings.

## [ECO4-TECH-04] ECO4 Fabric-First Solid Wall & Cavity Wall Insulation
Solid brick or uninsulated cavity wall dwellings must prioritise fabric insulation (external/internal solid wall insulation or cavity fill) before or alongside clean heating (air source heat pump) upgrades under ECO4 multi-measure packages.
